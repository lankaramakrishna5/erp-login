ERP One-Click Login

Log in to IIT Kharagpur ERP with one click in Firefox — roll number, password, security answer and the email OTP, all filled in for you.

Open this page in Firefox on your laptop or PC. This add-on is for Firefox only.

Using Chrome? Use the original Chrome version instead: github.com/amanjuneja420/erp-one-click-login (follow its Install steps).

This works on Firefox for laptops and PCs, not phones. Open this link on your computer.

Install for Firefox

Version 1.4.0 · updates install automatically

1. Install (3 clicks)

  1. Click Install for Firefox above.
  2. Firefox says it “prevented this site from asking you to install software” — click Continue to Installation.
  3. Click Add, then Okay.

2. First login (once)

  1. In Firefox, sign in to Gmail with the inbox that gets your ERP OTPs.
  2. Open erp.iitkgp.ac.in and click the ⚡ One-Click Login button in the bottom-right corner.
  3. Type your roll number, password and security answer like always, then click Get OTP.
  4. Click OK when it asks to remember you. It reads the OTP from Gmail and logs you in.

ERP asks one of your 3 security questions at random. The first time it asks one the add-on hasn't seen, just type that answer once and it's remembered.

3. After that: one click

Click ⚡ One-Click Login on the ERP login page, click the add-on's icon → Log in to ERP, or press Alt+Shift+E anywhere in Firefox. It never logs in by itself — only when you ask.

Good to know

  • Your roll number, password and answers are stored only in your Firefox and sent only to ERP. No server, no tracking.
  • The OTP is read from Gmail's unread-mail preview; the email isn't opened and stays unread.
  • Anyone using your unlocked computer could log in to your ERP. Use it only on your own laptop, not lab or shared PCs.
  • Remove any other ERP auto-login add-on — two of them on the login page clash.
  • Something not working? Click the add-on's icon: it shows what happened on the last attempt.
